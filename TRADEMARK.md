# Trademark policy

The code is open source under Apache-2.0: you may use, copy, modify and
redistribute it. **The name "okproof" and its logo are not part of that
license** (Apache-2.0, section 6). They identify the official project, so
people can trust that something called okproof comes from it.

This policy follows the approach of projects such as Rust, Node.js and
Kubernetes.

## You may, without asking

- Say your project "works with okproof", "uses okproof" or "supports okproof proof
  files".
- Write articles, talks, tutorials and courses about okproof.
- Use the name to refer to the official project and its official releases.
- Keep the name in an unmodified copy you redistribute, with the NOTICE file.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a fork, product, service, package or company "okproof", or anything
  confusingly similar.
- Publish modified builds under the okproof name (for example an npm package
  called `okproof-something` that ships changed okproof code).
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement or official status.
- Offer a hosted service called okproof.

## Forks

Forking is welcome. Give your fork a different name and logo, and say it's
"based on okproof".

## Official releases

The only official packages are the ones published from
[github.com/rudr19/okproof](https://github.com/rudr19/okproof) by its release
workflow, with npm provenance. Check provenance with `npm audit signatures`.

Open an issue, or contact [@rudr19](https://github.com/rudr19).
